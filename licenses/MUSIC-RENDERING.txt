Music rendering provenance

Source MIDI: the original game's title.mid, bgm01.mid, bgm02.mid, bgm03.mid,
bgm04.mid and boss01.mid, preserved in assets/audio.

Offline renderer: FluidSynth 2.6.1 Windows x64
https://github.com/FluidSynth/fluidsynth/releases/tag/v2.6.1
Soundfont: GeneralUser GS 2.0.3 by S. Christian Collins
https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser.php
https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS
License: GENERALUSER-GS-LICENSE.txt in this directory.

Render settings: 44,100 Hz stereo, gain 0.4, floating-point intermediate WAV.
Converted to Ogg Vorbis using the renderer's bundled libsndfile library.
Tracks with PCM peaks above 0.98 were attenuated to avoid clipping.
Conversion reports are included below. GeneralUser GS, FluidSynth and libsndfile
binaries are build tools and are not shipped or required by the game.
Original MIDI instrumentation and arrangement are retained, but the sampled
instrument timbres differ from historical Windows GM/GS playback devices.
