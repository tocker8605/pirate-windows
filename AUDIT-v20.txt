1. Restore v18 regular enemy visuals

   animation are active again. The v15 boss atlas, v18 fist orientation,

2. Centre single muzzle locations
   Kinds3,7,11 now emit from local X25 rather than X20. Shared single_muzzle()
   also aligns the kind22 preparation charge with actual projectile origin.
   Projectile types, timing gates, source-coordinate modulo rules, original
   non-normalized aim and dual/alternating launchers retain their algorithms.

3. Supply craft carries a detachable power item
   New carrier-v20.png is an empty mechanical grabber. One green power-item
   sprite is drawn inside it for both approach201 and vulnerable19 states.
   The same cargo_position() anchor(25,40) is used by mount, spawn and VFX.
   A successful hit removes mounted cargo, emits one green release burst,
   creates one falling reward and lets the empty craft escape. The reward
   smoothly grows from mounted scale0.6 to normal scale1 over12 authored units.
   Existing open-crossing detection, score and duplicate-reward protection stay.
   The old dot decoration is no longer painted over renewed supply craft.

4. Boss low-health smoke
   Living boss bodies with HP strictly below40percent of actual max_hp emit
   dense black smoke at three body/shoulder ports. Particle size16, cadence,
   lifetime28 and current-scroll advection match large enemy damage smoke.
   Exactly40percent and dead boss bodies do not emit this damage trail.

5. Fixed game speed
   GAME SPEED label, numeric value and selectable menu row removed.
   Options begin at GAME LEVEL, followed by all three weapons.
   Runtime always integrates level1 speed with native60 gameplay ticks/sec.
   Historical timing helpers retain optional parameters for regression fixtures.
